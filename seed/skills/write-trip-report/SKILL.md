# Writing a trip report

## Inputs
Everything under `/notes/`, plus the plans under `/plan/`. If `/notes/` is empty,
say so and offer to write a forward-looking plan instead of inventing content.

## Structure
1. **What we went for** — one paragraph, from `/AGENTS.md`.
2. **What we learned** — three to five items, each tied to a session ID.
3. **What we should do** — concrete actions with an owner shape, not vague intent.
4. **What we skipped and why** — honesty here is what makes the rest credible.

## Rules
- Never attribute a claim to a speaker unless it is in the notes.
- Sessions with no notes are listed as attended-without-notes, not summarised
  from the abstract.
- Keep it under one page. A manager who reads all of it beats a report nobody finishes.
